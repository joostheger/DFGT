'Seats of Power - Grand Republic' is part of the Seats of Power mod set. 

# Seats of Power #
Seats of Power adds multiple new government types for vanilla dwarves in Dwarf Fortress. Each government introduces its own mechanics and gameplay focus.
The submods are mutually compatible and designed to work with vanilla dwarves without altering too much of the game's core systems.

## Design Philosophy ##
Every government in Seats of Power is built around one or two defining, central gameplay mechanics, paired with a fitting theme. All other characteristics are shaped to match that theme, creating a cohesive blend of fantasy and history in every set.

Seats of Power focuses on entity-level modding — government structure, positions, and social mechanics — rather than new creatures, buildings, items or reactions. Additional modding is only used where it genuinely reinforces a theme, such as slavery in the Slavers Pact.

The mod set is built entirely on raw-modding possibilities, so DFHack is not required. It does add a nice touch through generic position names, but remains entirely optional.

Finally, Seats of Power deliberately introduces no new races. Every government is designed for the dwarves we all know and love.

## Governments Included ##
* Grand Republic - An aristocratic government centered on trade, influence, and rigid social hierarchy.
* League of Dukes - A confederation of cities, governed by a council of dukes and aldermen as elected officials. Upon reaching certain population thresholds, the League promotes a grand duke to archduke.
* Slavers' Pact - A federation of ruthless, decadent masters, applying systematic slavery and exploitation.
* Raider Clans - A tribal society that survives through raiding and pillaging neighboring settlements, based on familiar clan structures.
* Free Folk - A decentralized, informal government where individual dwarves assume administrative and leadership roles as the need arises, without expecting honour or recognition in return.

Not playable:
* City-State - A government centered around a single, dominant city, with authority radiating outward to surrounding territories, lead by an Archon and supported by a council.
  
Not yet released:
* Holy Order of the Tree - A tree-venerating military order that aggressively spreads its beliefs.
* Mountain Monk Republic - A theocratic government focused on religious devotion and communal living.
* Mountainborne Empire - The opus magnum of Seats of Power: a vast and intricate government that merges elements of all other types into a single, awe-inspiring imperial system.
* Mageocracy - A government ruled by powerful mages who use their arcane abilities to maintain control and influence over their subjects.
* Cult of the God-King - A government that revolves around the worship of a single, immortal dwarf, with a focus on dark rituals and ceremonies.
