'Seats of Power - Grand Republic' is part of the Seats of Power mod set. It adds multiple new government types for vanilla dwarves in Dwarf Fortress. Each government introduces its own mechanics and gameplay focus.
All governments are mutually compatible and designed to work with vanilla dwarves without altering to much of the game's core systems.

The set includes:
* Grand Republic - An aristocratic government centered on trade, influence, and rigid social hierarchy.
* Holy Order of the Tree - A tree-venerating military order that aggressively spreads its beliefs.
* Mountain Monk Republic - A theocratic government focused on religious devotion and communal living.
* Slavers Pact - A ruthless regime built on systematic slavery and exploitation.
* Raider Clans - A tribal society that survives through raiding and pillaging rival settlements, based on familiar clan structures.
* Mountainborne Empire - The opus magnum of Seats of Power: a vast and intricate government that merges elements of all other types into a single, awe-inspiring imperial system.
* Mageocracy - A government ruled by powerful mages who use their arcane abilities to maintain control and influence over their subjects.

Seats of Power also includes some more experimental and unconventional government types that offer unique gameplay experiences:
* Free Folk - A decentralized government where individual dwarves have more autonomy and freedom, with less emphasis on hierarchy, competition and control and more on nature, friendship, and cooporation. 
* Anarchists Commune - A government that rejects all forms of authority and hierarchy, where dwarves live in a communal society based on mutual aid and cooperation, with a focus on self-governance and direct democracy.
* Sacrificial Cult - A government that revolves around the worship of a dark deity, where dwarves are sacrificed to appease their god and maintain their favor, with a focus on dark rituals and ceremonies.
