

## General features or the Grand Republic ##
* The grand dwarven republics are peaceful, trade focused civilizations, with a strong focus on commerce and cooperation.
* Towns and Hamlets are build the fertile plains of your world, embracing the mountains where their kin lives.
* The Merchants Guilds is in charge of the republic, sending their representatives as diplomats to the fortresses. 
* They pray to the gods of trade, wealth and order.
* Their peacefull nature of avoiding conflic makes them a good trading partner.
* Republican dwarfs value competition and hardwork, cooperation and commerce over martial prowess and merriment.
* Caravans will arrive in spring, summer and autumn. 
* The options for production of clothing and weapons are expanded and they make use of all type of crops
* Specified naming voor groups, roads, bridges, etc.


## Guide for players ##
* They main feature of this mod is the **extended citizenry system**. It gives all 7 starting dwarves a special status, granting the rights of extensive demands. The progress from *settler* to *citizen*, *burger* and even *patrician*.
When reaching certain thresholds, they are automatically promoted. When you receive message of such a promotion, check the nobles screen for new demands. 
* Read the description of the positions for more details on the requirements and benefits of each position.  
* Once reaching a certain status, members of the citizenry can no longer be assigned tasks. They are exempt from menial labour. However, any taks that they had been assigned before, are still being done. It can by wise to check this early, and only give certain tasks for them to do. When they have the need to craft something, you can consider assign that labor to them, so they can get busy now and then.
* This intricate promotion system has unfortunately some drawbacks:
  * units get multiple sequential positions, resulting in multiple messages. Their status screen also mentions the history of these promotion paths.
  * in world-gen, this system doesnt work. therefore there are dedicated positions created, to give world-gen sites a representation of the fortress-mode system. 
* Clerks are be able to appoint from certain ranks of the citizenry for tasks like trading (clerk of stores) management (clerk of works), accounting (clerk of accounts). Even more of these positions will be opend up when the settlement grows.
* Their lack of military focus makes them limited in their options for building up an army. But when their settlements grows, new options will open up.
* If a diplomat leaves unhappy, that might be one of the two merchant journeymen who arrive with the caravan. You probably didn't had a Clerk of stores assigned. Don't be alarmed, the only thing you missed was an supplemental trade negotiation. 
* There is a functional noble position, called Promotion Trigger. This position has no special features, but can be used to trigger in the game that the noblity postions are evaluated, and setting promotion in motion, if aplied.

## Starting a new fortress ##
* When starting a new fortress, select a civilization with the name '... Republic ...' and that had the 'Honorary Grand Master' as the leader of the civilization. 
* On embark, before you unpause, check the noble screen for the position of 'select broker' and read its decription. 
* Dont worry about missing nobles positions. When you let your fortress grow, new positions will open up automatically.
