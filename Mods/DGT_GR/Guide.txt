* Once reaching a certain status, members of the citizenry can no longer be assigned tasks. They are exempt from menial labour. However, any taks that they had been assigned before, are still applied. It can by wise to check this early, and only give 'save' tasks for them to do. When they have the need to craft something, you can consider assign that labor to them, so they can get there hands dirty now and then.
* If a diplomat leaves unhappy, that might be one of the two merchant journeymen who arrive with the caravan. You probably didn't had a Clerk of stores assigned. Don't be alarmed, the only thing you missed was a trade negotiation.
