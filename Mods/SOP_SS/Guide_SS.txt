* Settings: set your population cap anything between 20 (minimum for strange moods) and 50 dwarves. Because non of them will work, its very hard to manage a fortress with only slaves. 
* Once reaching a certain status, free people can no longer be assigned tasks. They are exempt from menial labour. However, any taks that they had been assigned before, are still applied. It can by wise to check this early, and only give 'save' tasks for them to do. When they have the need to craft something, you can consider assign that labor to them, so they can get there hands dirty now and then.
